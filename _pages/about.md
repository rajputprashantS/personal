I am a bioinformatics researcher with a background in computational biology and a strong interest in using technology to solve complex biological problems. I completed my Master’s in Bioinformatics and am currently pursuing a PhD in the Department of Biotechnology at IIT Hyderabad. I enjoy exploring new technologies, solving challenging problems, and developing computational tools to address biological questions. I believe in continuous learning, creativity, and collaboration, and I am always eager to explore new ideas and opportunities for growth.
